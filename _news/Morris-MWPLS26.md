The ninth Midwest Programming Languages Summit (MWPLS'26) will take place on Friday, November 6th, 2026, hosted by the University of Iowa, in Iowa City.

MWPLS is an informal workshop that is intended to foster the exchange of ideas and to promote collaboration among PL researchers in the greater midwest. For more details, or to submit a talk or poster proposal, please visit [the MWPLS website](https://2026.mwpls.org).
